Frequently asked questions

How fresh is the produce, really?

Everything is picked or collected the morning it ships. Leafy greens and berries move through a cold chain at 4°C, so what you unpack is the same grade we packed.

What are the delivery charges?

Delivery is ₹29 on orders below ₹499, and free above that. Express delivery within 90 minutes is available in selected areas at no extra cost.

What if an item arrives damaged?

Tell us within 24 hours with a photo and we refund that item in full — no return pickup, no arguments.

Which payment methods do you accept?

UPI, credit and debit cards, and cash on delivery. Refunds go back to the original method within 3–5 working days.

Can I order in bulk for a restaurant or office?

Yes. Write to care@velorafresh.example with your weekly volumes and we will set up a standing order with wholesale pricing.

Do you have a minimum order value?

No minimum. Order a single bunch of spinach if that is all you need.
