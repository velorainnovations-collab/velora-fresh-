We are Velora Fresh

A small supermarket with one obsession — produce that still tastes like it came from a farm.

Our story

Velora Fresh started with a simple frustration: vegetables that were three days old by the time they reached a shelf, and fruit priced as though it had travelled first class. We thought a shorter route was possible.

So we built one. We buy directly from growers in the surrounding districts, grade everything by hand at our packhouse the same morning, and drive it to your door before the day is out. No warehouse queue, no wholesale middleman, no mystery about where your food came from.

Today Velora Fresh serves thousands of households every week — and we still know the name of every farm on our list.

How we source

Four checkpoints between the field and your kitchen.

1

Direct from growers

Fixed, fair prices agreed with farms each season — no auction floor.

2

Same-morning grading

Every crate is sorted by hand. Bruised or under-ripe stock never ships.

3

Cold chain packing

Leafy greens and berries stay at 4°C right up to the doorstep.

4

Delivered the same day

Short routes, small vans, and slots you actually choose.

What we stand for

🌿 Freshness over everything

If it would not go in our own kitchen, it does not go in your basket. Anything that arrives below standard is refunded in full.

🤝 Fair to farmers

Growers are paid within seven days at a rate agreed before the season, not after the harvest glut.

♻️ Less waste

Returnable crates, compostable liners, and surplus produce donated to community kitchens each week.
